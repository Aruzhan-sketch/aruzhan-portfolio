## 📋 How to Use This Portfolio

### For Employers & Collaborators
- Start with **README.md** for an overview
- Review **cv.md** for full professional details
- Check **projects/** for current research
- Visit **contact.md** to get in touch

### For Students & Colleagues
- Browse **blog/** for articles and insights
- Explore **projects/** to learn about research interests
- Check **cv.md** for teaching experience and credentials

### For Academic Networks
- Review research projects and publications
- Explore teaching experience and methodology interests
- Connect via contact page for collaboration

## 🎨 Design Principles

This portfolio emphasizes:
- **Clarity**: Clear organization and easy navigation
- **Authenticity**: Genuine voice and real experiences
- **Professionalism**: Academic and career-focused content
- **Accessibility**: Information available in multiple formats
- **Engagement**: Inviting discussion and collaboration

## 🔄 Maintenance

- Update CV with new accomplishments and experiences
- Add blog posts regularly to share insights
- Add research updates to projects section
- Keep contact information current
